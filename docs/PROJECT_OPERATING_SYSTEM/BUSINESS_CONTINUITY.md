# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the Business Continuity framework for the Project
Operating System. It establishes deterministic governance for maintaining
repository availability, engineering operations, recovery readiness, and
service continuity during planned or unplanned disruptions while preserving
the frozen architecture.

---

# 2. OBJECTIVES

Business Continuity shall:

- Maintain engineering operations.
- Minimize service disruption.
- Preserve repository integrity.
- Ensure recovery readiness.
- Maintain engineering traceability.
- Support engineering audits.
- Preserve deterministic execution.

Business Continuity applies to:

# 6. CONTINUITY RESPONSIBILITIES

Continuity Planning

- Define continuity objectives.
- Identify critical engineering services.
- Establish continuity procedures.

Risk Assessment

- Identify continuity risks.
- Evaluate operational impact.
- Prioritize recovery activities.

Recovery Preparation

- Maintain recovery procedures.
- Validate backup availability.
- Prepare recovery resources.

Continuity Activation

- Initiate approved continuity procedures.
- Assign engineering responsibilities.
- Record activation metadata.

Operational Recovery

- Restore engineering operations.
- Recover repository services.
- Preserve repository integrity.

Repository Verification

- Validate repository consistency.
- Verify engineering assets.
- Confirm documentation integrity.

Service Restoration

- Resume normal engineering operations.
- Verify service availability.
- Record restoration activities.

Continuity Validation

- Confirm recovery objectives.
- Validate operational readiness.
- Record validation outcomes.

Continuous Improvement

- Review continuity effectiveness.
- Record improvement actions.
- Update continuity procedures.
