# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the Risk Management framework for the Project
Operating System. It establishes deterministic governance for identifying,
assessing, mitigating, monitoring, and documenting repository risks while
preserving the frozen architecture.

---

# 2. OBJECTIVES

Risk Management shall:

- Identify repository risks.
- Assess engineering impact.
- Define mitigation strategies.
- Preserve repository stability.
- Maintain engineering traceability.
- Support engineering audits.
- Ensure deterministic execution.

---

# 3. RISK SOURCES

Repository risks may originate from:

- Software defects.
- Dependency changes.
- Infrastructure failures.
- Security vulnerabilities.
- Performance degradation.
- Documentation inconsistencies.
- Operational failures.
- Human error.

---

# 4. RISK MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

Every repository risk shall:

- Be formally documented.
- Be assigned an owner.
- Be evaluated consistently.
- Include mitigation actions.
- Be continuously monitored.
- Preserve frozen architecture.
- Maintain complete engineering traceability.


---

# 6. RISK RESPONSIBILITIES

Risk Identification

- Identify repository risks.
- Record risk source.
- Assign a unique risk identifier.

Risk Assessment

- Evaluate probability.
- Assess business impact.
- Determine engineering severity.

Risk Classification

- Categorize risk type.
- Assign priority level.
- Record classification metadata.

Mitigation Planning

- Define mitigation strategy.
- Assign responsible owner.
- Establish completion criteria.

Mitigation Implementation

- Execute approved mitigation.
- Preserve repository integrity.
- Record implementation activities.

Risk Monitoring

- Continuously monitor risk status.
- Track mitigation effectiveness.
- Update monitoring records.

Risk Validation

- Verify mitigation success.
- Confirm repository stability.
- Record validation results.

Risk Closure

- Close resolved risks.
- Archive mitigation records.
- Preserve engineering traceability.

Historical Retention

- Preserve historical risk records.
- Maintain append-only history.
- Support engineering audits.

# 9. RISK REGISTER POLICY

The Project Operating System shall maintain a permanent Risk Register.

The Risk Register shall:

- Preserve every identified risk.
- Maintain chronological ordering.
- Never overwrite historical records.
- Record unique risk identifiers.
- Record ownership and status.
- Preserve engineering traceability.
- Support repository audits.
- Maintain append-only historical records.
