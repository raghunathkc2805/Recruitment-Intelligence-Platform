# 1. PURPOSE

This document maintains the chronological history of engineering sprints for
the Recruitment Intelligence Platform.

Sprint history provides complete engineering traceability while preserving the
frozen architecture.

The document shall be generated automatically from machine-readable project
state.

---

# 2. SPRINT MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

Sprint history shall:

- Preserve chronological ordering.
- Maintain immutable historical records.
- Support engineering audits.
- Preserve deterministic generation.
- Record engineering milestones.
- Maintain complete traceability.
- Preserve backward compatibility.
